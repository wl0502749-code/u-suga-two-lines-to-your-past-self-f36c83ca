Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca?ref=4rzbqkgp) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun/?ref=4rzbqkgp).

`main.py` writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: line one warns the past
self about one specific moment, and line two says in one sentence why that
moment mattered and what changed because of it.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The entry is validated to be UTF-8 text of 1–280 characters, not only
whitespace.
